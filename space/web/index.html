Leave the launch outpost for Morrow's green highlands or Ember's mineral ridges. Pilot the starter hull, park to customize it, and switch main power off to recharge from your solar reserve. Your hull and terrain are saved on this device.

Browser-only solo world · Not yet connected to native Luanti servers

Controls & settings

Touch: left stick to move, swipe the world to look. Hold arrows to rise/fall. Aim the center crosshair; hold Build or Remove.

Keyboard: WASD · Space up · Ctrl/C down · Shift boost · R rotate. Mouse lock where supported. iPad trackpad: drag to look, short click removes, secondary click builds. F builds, X removes, V pilots/exits. Escape opens this menu. Touch and keyboard use the same flight actions.

Navigation & shipyard

Board within 12 m. Fly forward along your view; arrows rise/fall. Main power OFF disables thrust and transfers the solar reserve into main batteries. Add battery blocks for capacity; exposed solar chargers refill the reserve. Solar chargers need a clear upward view of the sun. Switch Terrain → Hull to edit your parked ship. All hull blocks stay attached to the core. This bounded sector has no gravity, orbital simulation or online multiplayer yet.

Saves stay in this browser. Keep a backup before clearing Safari data. After your first visit, offline play is available once the page says “Offline ready”. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
