# Asset provenance

The eight `mods/space_core/textures/space_*.png` files are original geometric
textures generated by `space/tools/generate_textures.py` (Pillow). They use no
external images, proprietary references, or generative image assets. They include
four material tiles, a ghost tile, a tool icon and two temporary pilot sprites.
License: CC0-1.0.

No third-party game assets are included. Luanti's existing interface textures,
fonts, engine and renderer retain upstream credits and licenses in `LICENSE.txt`,
the upstream source headers, and their respective directories.

Add every future external asset here with its source URL, author, license,
modifications, and included file paths before committing it.
